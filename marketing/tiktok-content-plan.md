# SonoPass TikTok content plan

Goal: get sonography students to download SonoPass and try a free Quick 10.
Audience: sonography students and new grads studying for the SPI, RVT and specialty registries
(mostly 20–35, studying on their phone between classes, clinicals and shifts).

---

## The rules every video follows

These come from Harry Dry's copywriting rules at Marketing Examples. Every line of copy has to pass three
tests:

1. **Can I visualize it?** Show the thing. Not "aliasing is a Doppler error". Show the spectral waveform
   wrapping over the top and coming back from the bottom.
2. **Can I falsify it?** Say something specific and checkable. Not "the best SPI prep". Say
   "110 questions, 2 hours, timed exactly like the real SPI".
3. **Can nobody else say it?** Only a sonography-focused brand can say "the 13-microsecond rule" or
   "your PRF is too low for that jet". Generic study-motivation content can come from anyone, so skip it.

And from the same school of thinking:

- **Specific beats vague.** "9 registries, one price" beats "everything you need".
- **Show, don't tell.** Film the app answering the question, not someone saying the app is great.
- **The hook is the first second.** Open on the problem or the question, never on "Hey guys".
- **One video, one idea.** If you need "and also", it's two videos.
- **Write like you talk.** Read every script out loud; cut any word you wouldn't say to a classmate.
- **Steal your audience's words.** Use the phrases students use in comments and DMs ("physics is
  killing me", "I keep mixing up axial and lateral") as hooks.

## Five content pillars

| Pillar | What it is | Why it works | Share of posts |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. **"Can you get this one?"** | One registry-style question on screen, 3-second pause, then the answer and why | People stop to test themselves, comment their answer, and rewatch | 35% |
| 2. **Physics in 30 seconds** | One SPI concept explained with a visual you can picture (hands, objects, the app) | SPI physics is the #1 fear; a clear picture is shareable | 25% |
| 3. **Mistakes that cost points** | "Stop doing X on the SPI", common mix-ups (axial vs lateral, PRF vs frequency) | Loss-framed, specific, saves people from errors | 15% |
| 4. **Study system / exam day** | How to study in 5-minute gaps, what to bring to Pearson VUE, pacing | Practical, saveable, ties naturally to Quick 10 and timed mocks | 15% |
| 5. **The offer** | All 9 registries in one plan; Black Friday Lifetime; free daily Quick 10 | Converts the audience the other 85% built | 10% |

Rule: four value videos for every one that asks for the download.

## Formats (all can be filmed on a phone)

- **Green screen over the app.** You in the corner, a SonoPass question or result screen behind you.
- **Screen recording + voiceover.** Record a Quick 10 question in the app, talk over it.
- **Text-only quiz.** Question on screen, a ticking 3-2-1, answer reveal. No face needed.
- **Hands demo.** Use your hands, a slinky, a pen, or a whiteboard to picture a physics idea.
- **Photo carousel (TikTok photo mode).** 5–7 slides: "7 SPI formulas on one page". Highly saveable.
- **Stitch / reply to comments.** Answer a student's question with a new video. It tells TikTok the video
  is a conversation, and tells the student you listen.

**Never film real patient images** unless they're fully de-identified and you have the right to use them.
Use the app, diagrams, phantoms or your hands instead.

## 10 ready-to-film scripts

On-screen text is in quotes. Each runs 20–45 seconds.

### 1. The 13-microsecond rule (Physics in 30 seconds)
- **Hook (0–2s):** "The SPI loves this number: 13."
- **Beat 1:** "Sound goes about 1540 meters per second in soft tissue."
- **Beat 2:** "That means every 1 centimeter of depth costs about 13 microseconds of round-trip time."
- **Beat 3 (visual: point to the screen depth scale):** "Echo comes back after 26 microseconds? The
  reflector is 2 centimeters deep. 65 microseconds? 5 centimeters."
- **Payoff:** "Double the depth, double the time. That's the range equation without the math."
- **CTA:** "Want 10 more like this in 5 minutes? Free daily Quick 10 in SonoPass. Link in bio."
- **Caption:** "13 µs per cm of depth. Remember that and half the range-equation questions are free. #SPI
  #sonographystudent #ultrasoundphysics"

### 2. Aliasing in one picture (Physics in 30 seconds)
- **Hook:** "Your Doppler waveform is wrapping around the screen. Here's why."
- **Visual:** a spectral trace that goes off the top and reappears at the bottom (draw it or screen-grab
  from a textbook diagram you have rights to).
- **Beat 1:** "The machine can only measure shifts up to half the PRF. That's the Nyquist limit."
- **Beat 2:** "Go above it and the peak gets cut off and shows up on the wrong side."
- **Fixes (fast list on screen):** "Raise the scale (PRF). Drop the baseline. Make it shallower. Use a
  lower-frequency transducer. Or switch to continuous wave."
- **CTA:** "Save this for the SPI."
- **Caption:** "Nyquist limit = PRF ÷ 2. Above it, you alias. #ultrasound #doppler #SPIexam"

### 3. "Can you get this one?" Axial resolution (Quiz)
- **On screen:** "Which change improves AXIAL resolution? A) Wider beam B) Shorter pulse
  C) Deeper focus D) Lower frequency"
- **Pause:** "3… 2… 1…"
- **Answer:** "B. Shorter pulse. Axial resolution = half the spatial pulse length. Fewer cycles or higher
  frequency makes the pulse shorter."
- **Trap:** "If you picked A, you were thinking of lateral resolution. That's beam width."
- **CTA:** "Comment your answer before you watched. 3,200+ more in SonoPass."
- **Caption:** "Axial = along the beam, depends on pulse length. Lateral = side to side, depends on beam
  width. #SPI #sonography"

### 4. Stop mixing up PRF and frequency (Mistakes that cost points)
- **Hook:** "If you think PRF and transducer frequency are the same thing, the SPI will eat you."
- **Beat 1 (hands far apart):** "Frequency is how many cycles per second inside the pulse. Megahertz.
  Set by the crystal."
- **Beat 2 (hands clapping slowly):** "PRF is how many pulses per second the machine sends. Kilohertz.
  Set by depth."
- **Beat 3:** "Go deeper and PRF has to drop, because the machine waits longer to listen."
- **CTA:** "Practice this in Study Mode, by category. Free Quick 10 daily."
- **Caption:** "Frequency = cycles per second (MHz). PRF = pulses per second (kHz). Deeper = lower PRF."

### 5. Mirror image artifact (Quiz)
- **On screen:** "You see a second copy of the liver ABOVE the diaphragm. What is it?"
- **Pause:** "3… 2… 1…"
- **Answer:** "Mirror image. A strong reflector, the diaphragm, bounces the beam. The machine assumes a
  straight path, so it places the copy deeper, on the other side of the reflector."
- **CTA:** "SonoPass has a whole SPI Artifact Drill. 24 questions."
- **Caption:** "The copy is always deeper than the real thing, across the strong reflector. #ultrasound
  #artifacts #SPI"

### 6. How to study for the SPI in 5-minute gaps (Study system)
- **Hook:** "I don't have 2 hours to study. I have 5 minutes between clinical cases."
- **Visual:** start a Quick 10 in the app, answer one, open the explanation.
- **Beat 1:** "10 questions. About 5 minutes. Read every explanation, even the ones you got right."
- **Beat 2:** "Three of those a day is 30 questions, about 200 a week, without a study night." (One Quick 10 a day is free; more sessions need Pro.)
- **Beat 3:** "The app tracks which categories you keep missing, so the next session hits those."
- **CTA:** "Your first Quick 10 every day is free."
- **Caption:** "5 minutes, 10 questions, every explanation. Repeat. #sonographystudent #studytips"

### 7. What to expect at Pearson VUE (Study system / exam day)

### 8. The SPI timing math (Study system)

  (swap in `#RVT #vascularultrasound` etc. for specialty videos).
- Bio: "Pass your registry. 3,200+ questions, 9 registries. Free daily Quick 10 👇" + link to
  `https://sonopass.com/#download`.

## Cadence and what to measure

- **Post 1 a day for 30 days**, then keep what works. Batch-film 7 videos in one sitting.
- Reply to every comment in the first hour; turn the best questions into "reply to comment" videos.
- Watch three numbers per video: **average watch time / % watched**, **saves**, **profile visits**.
  Double down on the pillar with the most saves (saves = "I'll study this later" = future download).
- Every Sunday: look at the top 3 videos, film 3 variations of the winner's hook.

## Repurpose the blog

Each blog post = 5+ videos. Pull one section per video:
- "SPI formulas" post → 7 photo-carousel slides + 5 "Physics in 30 seconds".
- "How to fix aliasing" post → aliasing explainer + "5 fixes" list video + quiz.
- "Artifacts" post → one quiz per artifact (10+ videos).
- "Exam day tips" post → Pearson VUE video + night-before video.

## Guardrails

  affiliated with ARDMS.
