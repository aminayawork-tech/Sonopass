Why these words: Apple searches the name, subtitle and keywords together and combines words across
them, so nothing is repeated. "ultrasound", "exam", "prep", "sonography", "registry" and "review"
are already in the name/subtitle; the keywords add the exam names students type (SPI, physics,
vascular, OB/GYN, echo…) and the study formats (mock, practice, test, flashcards). Together they cover
searches such as *SPI exam prep*, *ultrasound practice test*, *sonography mock exam*, *vascular
registry review*, *echo flashcards* and *sonographer exam*.

"ARDMS" and the credential names (RDMS, RDCS, RVT) are left out of the name, subtitle and keywords on
purpose: they are trademarks, and Apple's metadata rule (guideline 2.3.7) is a common rejection reason.
They appear only in the description, used descriptively alongside the "not affiliated" notice.
